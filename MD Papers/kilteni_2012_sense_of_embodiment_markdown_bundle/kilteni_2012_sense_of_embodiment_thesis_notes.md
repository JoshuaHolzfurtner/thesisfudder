- Paper: *The Sense of Embodiment in Virtual Reality*
- Authors: Konstantina Kilteni, Raphaela Groten, Mel Slater
- Journal: *Presence*, Vol. 21, No. 4, Fall 2012, pp. 373-387

## Why this paper matters for the thesis

This is a central definition/source paper for Sense of Embodiment (SoE) in VR. It is useful because it gives a working definition, separates SoE into three subcomponents, and discusses measurement/enhancement strategies for each component.

## Core definition to verify in the PDF

- **PDF page 3 / article page 375:** SoE is defined as the sense that emerges when a body B's properties are processed as if they were properties of one's own biological body.
- This is probably one of the safest anchor definitions for your thesis, but quote it only after checking the original PDF.

## Three SoE subcomponents

- **Self-location** — the spatial experience of being inside a body.
  - Main location in PDF: page 3-4.
  - Important distinction: self-location is about the self-body relation, not simply being present inside a virtual world.
- **Agency** — the sense of motor control / authorship over body actions.
  - Main location in PDF: page 4-5.
  - Strongly relevant to your erg-handle / avatar-control mapping.
- **Body ownership** — self-attribution of a body as one's own.
  - Main location in PDF: page 5.
  - Strongly relevant to avatar realism, morphology, hand/arm alignment, and ownership questionnaires.

## Direct relevance to XR rowing thesis

### Erg-centered/high-congruence mode

Likely maps strongly to agency and potentially ownership because the user's real handle/seat motion is closely matched to the virtual body. This fits the paper's emphasis on visuomotor and sensorimotor correlations, especially for agency.

### Boat-centered/sport-depiction mode

May preserve strong sport realism and first-person body perspective, but can reduce exact visuomotor congruence if the visual oar/hand trajectory differs from the real erg handle trajectory. This makes the paper useful for framing a tradeoff between agency/congruence and realistic sport depiction.

### Latency / timing

- **PDF page 5 / article page 377:** the paper mentions work showing that discrepancies between visual feedback and actual movement negatively affect agency, including a study where more than 150 ms reduced agency.
- Use this carefully: it is a cited finding within the review, not necessarily a universal VR rowing threshold.

## Measures mentioned

### Self-location measures

- Questionnaires
- Body-position estimation tasks
- Physiological response to threats toward perceived self-location

Main location: PDF page 8-9.

### Agency measures

- Questionnaire items such as feeling in control of the rubber hand
- The authors note that agency measurement is less developed than ownership/self-location
- They suggest task performance in motor tasks as a potentially valuable measure

Main location: PDF page 8-10.

### Ownership measures

- Questionnaire items
- Proprioceptive drift / body localization
- Body-part size estimation
- Physiological threat responses
- Physiological signal changes such as temperature

Main location: PDF page 9-10.

## Enhancement strategies

- **Self-location:** first-person perspective from the virtual body's eyes; congruent tactile/vestibular information.
- **Agency:** real-time or near-real-time mapping of participant motion to avatar motion; avoid temporal discrepancies.
- **Ownership:** sensory correlations, haptic/visuotactile or visuoproprioceptive matching, human-like morphology, and possibly individualized avatars.

Main location: PDF page 11.

## Do-not-overclaim warnings

- The authors explicitly say the relationship and weighting between the three SoE subcomponents is not well understood.
- Do not claim that self-location, agency, and ownership are independent in a strong empirical sense.
- Do not claim that maximizing visual realism alone maximizes embodiment.
- Do not treat threat responses or physiological measures as direct proof of ownership without questionnaire/behavioral support.
- Do not treat the 150 ms agency point as a strict universal cutoff for all VR embodiment setups.

## Good thesis phrasing direction

> Kilteni et al. conceptualize SoE as a multidimensional construct involving self-location, agency, and body ownership. This allows the rowing prototype to be analyzed not only in terms of visual realism, but also in terms of how closely the user's real rowing actions, perceived body position, and avatar morphology support embodiment.

## Suggested use in thesis structure

- **Theory chapter:** definition of SoE and subcomponents.
- **Implementation chapter:** explain which design variables target agency, self-location, and ownership.
- **Evaluation chapter:** structure questionnaire items around the three subcomponents.
- **Limitations chapter:** acknowledge that subcomponents may interact and that their weighting is not settled.
