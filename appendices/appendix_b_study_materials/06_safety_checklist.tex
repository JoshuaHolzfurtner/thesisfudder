\section{Safety Checklist}
\label{app:study_materials_safety_checklist}

\subsection*{Before Participation}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Confirm that the participant understands the task.
    \item Confirm that participation is voluntary.
    \item Ask about acute injuries or pain relevant to rowing.
    \item Ask about dizziness, strong VR sickness susceptibility, or motion sickness concerns.
    \item Ask whether moderate physical exertion is safe for the participant.
    \item Check that the rowing area is clear and the headset fit is stable.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Stop Criteria}

Stop or pause the session if the participant reports:

\begin{itemize}
    \item dizziness,
    \item nausea,
    \item pain,
    \item distress,
    \item unsafe discomfort,
    \item headset or ergometer fit problems,
    \item a request to stop.
\end{itemize}
