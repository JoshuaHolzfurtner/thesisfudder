\section{Participant Information: English Draft}
\label{app:study_materials_participant_information_english}

You are invited to take part in a short study about virtual reality rowing. In the study, you will row on a physical rowing ergometer while wearing a VR headset. You will try two different virtual rowing representations and answer short questionnaires about how each representation felt.

The study focuses on how the virtual rowing body and movement are experienced. There are no right or wrong answers. Please answer based on your own experience.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without giving a reason. Please tell the experimenter immediately if you feel dizzy, nauseous, uncomfortable, in pain, or unsafe.

The session may involve physical exertion. Please do not participate if rowing, VR headset use, or moderate physical exertion would be unsafe for you.
