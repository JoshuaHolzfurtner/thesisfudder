\section{Session Script: English Draft}
\label{app:study_materials_session_script_english}

\subsection*{Before Starting}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Welcome the participant.
    \item Explain the task in neutral terms.
    \item Confirm consent and safety screening.
    \item Explain that the participant may stop at any time.
    \item Fit the VR headset and check comfort.
    \item Calibrate the rowing setup.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Condition Procedure}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Start the assigned first condition.
    \item Allow the participant to familiarize themselves with the mapping.
    \item Begin the standardized rowing period.
    \item Stop the condition and remove or pause the headset as needed.
    \item Ask the participant to complete the per-condition questionnaire.
    \item Offer a short rest.
    \item Repeat the procedure for the second condition.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{After Both Conditions}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Ask the participant to complete the post-comparison questionnaire.
    \item Ask whether they want to add any comments.
    \item Thank the participant and debrief them.
\end{enumerate}
