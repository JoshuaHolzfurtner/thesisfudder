Unless changed before data collection, per-condition ratings will use a 7-point agreement scale:

\begin{quote}
1 = strongly disagree, 7 = strongly agree
\end{quote}

The VEQ ownership and agency items are the primary embodiment measures. Rowing-specific items are custom exploratory measures and must not be combined with VEQ scores.

The custom items are intended to separate embodied action congruence from sport-action depiction. They therefore ask about the real ergometer action, the real handle as a haptic anchor, virtual hand/oar mapping, rowing-on-water plausibility, stroke-coupled locomotion, and discomfort or mismatch. These constructs are thesis-specific and should be reported separately from the validated VEQ subscales.
