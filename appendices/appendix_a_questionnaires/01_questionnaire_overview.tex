Unless changed before data collection, per-condition ratings will use a 7-point agreement scale:

\begin{quote}
1 = strongly disagree, 7 = strongly agree
\end{quote}

The VEQ ownership and agency items are the primary embodiment measures. Rowing-specific items are custom exploratory measures and must not be combined with VEQ scores.

The custom items are intended to separate embodied action congruence from sport-action depiction. They therefore ask about the real ergometer action, the real handle as a physical anchor, virtual hand/oar mapping, rowing-on-water plausibility, and perceived mismatch between the felt handle movement and the seen virtual movement. These constructs are thesis-specific and should be reported separately from the validated VEQ subscales.

Safety and comfort checks are recorded separately from the questionnaire constructs. Reports of dizziness, nausea, pain, or unsafe discomfort are relevant for stopping rules, feasibility, and limitations, but they should not be combined with VEQ or rowing-specific exploratory scores.
