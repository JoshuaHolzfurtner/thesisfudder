\section{Per-Condition Questionnaire: English Draft}
\label{app:questionnaires_per_condition_english}

Participants complete this questionnaire after each rowing condition.

\subsection*{Embodiment: Ownership}

\drafttodo{TODO:VERIFY these VEQ items against the original VEQ questionnaire sheet before data collection.}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item It felt like the virtual body was my body.
    \item It felt like the virtual body parts were my body parts.
    \item The virtual body felt like a human body.
    \item It felt like the virtual body belonged to me.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Embodiment: Agency}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item The movements of the virtual body felt like they were my movements.
    \item I felt like I was controlling the movements of the virtual body.
    \item I felt like I was causing the movements of the virtual body.
    \item The movements of the virtual body were in sync with my own movements.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Exploratory Body Location}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item I felt as if my body was located where I saw the virtual rowing body.
    \item I felt as if my hands were located where I saw the virtual hands.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Rowing-Specific Exploratory Items}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item The virtual rowing body felt connected to the movement I performed on the real ergometer.
    \item The real handle I was holding fit with the virtual hands, handle, or oars I saw.
    \item The real handle I was holding made the virtual rowing action feel physically grounded.
    \item The virtual hand and arm movement made sense in relation to my real handle movement.
    \item The virtual oar or rowing motion felt like a controllable extension of my own action.
    \item The representation felt believable as rowing on water.
    \item The visible hand, handle, or oar path made sense for the rowing representation I saw.
    \item I noticed a conflict between the real handle movement and the virtual hand or oar movement.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Safety and Comfort Check}

\begin{quote}
Did this condition make you feel dizzy, nauseous, in pain, or unsafe? If yes, please tell the experimenter and briefly describe it.
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Open Comment}

\begin{quote}
What, if anything, felt especially convincing or mismatched about the relation between the real ergometer and the virtual rowing representation?
\end{quote}
