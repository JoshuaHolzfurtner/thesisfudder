\chapter{AI Assistance Statement}
\label{app:ai_assistance}

This appendix documents how AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Codex) were used in the research and writing of this thesis. It summarizes the nature of the assistance, how the outputs were verified, and the role of the author in reviewing and editing AI-generated content.

The repository includes an \texttt{ai\_logs/} folder for process notes about the use of AI and large language model tools during thesis work. These logs are intended to support transparency about drafting, restructuring, review, LaTeX maintenance, and citation-gap detection. They are not treated as thesis evidence or as a substitute for source verification.

The author remains responsible for the final text, scientific claims, study design, data collection, analysis, interpretation, and citations.
