\section{Data Handling and Scoring}
\label{sec:study_design_data_handling}

Questionnaire responses will be recorded per participant and per condition. The data sheet will include participant code, condition order, background variables, per-condition ratings, comparative preference answers, and optional open comments.

Participant names or direct identifiers should not be stored in the analysis table. If contact or consent information must be stored, it should be kept separately from questionnaire responses.

The VEQ Ownership/Acceptance score will be calculated as the mean of the selected ownership items. The VEQ Control/Agency score will be calculated as the mean of the selected agency items. If any VEQ item is adapted for wording, the adaptation must be documented in Appendix~\ref{app:questionnaires}.

Custom rowing-specific items will be scored and reported separately. Preference items will be analyzed after both conditions because they depend on direct comparison.
