\section{Data Handling and Scoring}
\label{sec:study_design_data_handling}

Questionnaire responses will be recorded per participant and per condition. The analysis table will include a participant code, condition order, background variables, per-condition ratings, comparative preference answers, and optional open comments. Condition order must be retained because the within-subject design makes order, learning, and fatigue possible interpretive factors.

Participant names or direct identifiers should not be stored in the analysis table. If contact or consent information must be stored, it should be kept separately from questionnaire responses.

The VEQ Ownership/Acceptance score will be calculated as the mean of the selected ownership items. The VEQ Control/Agency score will be calculated as the mean of the selected agency items. If any VEQ item is adapted for wording, the adaptation must be documented in Appendix~\ref{app:questionnaires}. Exploratory body-location items will be reported separately because they are not part of the final VEQ factor structure used as the primary measure.

Custom rowing-specific items will be scored and reported separately from the VEQ. Preference items will be analyzed after both conditions because they depend on direct comparison. Open comments will be used to contextualize questionnaire patterns, especially where participants describe concrete sources of congruence, mismatch, comfort, or confusion.
