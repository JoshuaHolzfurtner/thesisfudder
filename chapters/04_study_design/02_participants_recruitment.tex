\section{Participants and Recruitment}
\label{sec:study_design_participants}

Participants will be recruited for a short XR rowing evaluation session. Because the study evaluates an early prototype and a specific representation trade-off, recruitment will be treated as exploratory rather than as a claim to represent all rowers, all VR users, or all exercise users. The final participant number, recruitment route, inclusion criteria, and exclusion criteria must be fixed before data collection is reported in past tense.

The study materials will record background variables that may influence how participants interpret the two representations. These include prior VR experience, prior rowing or ergometer experience, susceptibility to VR sickness or motion sickness, and any health or comfort restrictions relevant to rowing with a head-mounted display. These variables are not used to classify participants into experimental groups in the first study version, but they provide context for interpreting questionnaire responses and open comments.

Participants will receive the same explanation of the task and will be told that the study concerns their subjective experience of two virtual rowing representations. The instructions should avoid suggesting that one mode is expected to be better, more correct, or more realistic overall. This is important because the thesis does not treat ergometer congruence and boat realism as a simple hierarchy; it treats them as competing design priorities.
