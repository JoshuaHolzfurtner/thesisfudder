\section{Participants and Recruitment}
\label{sec:study_design_participants}

Participants will be recruited for a short XR rowing evaluation session. The target sample size, recruitment route, inclusion criteria, and exclusion criteria must be finalized before data collection is reported in past tense.

The study materials will record basic background variables that may influence the interpretation of the results. These include prior VR experience, prior rowing or ergometer experience, susceptibility to VR sickness or motion sickness, and any health or comfort restrictions relevant to rowing with a head-mounted display.

Participants will not be described as representative of all rowers, all VR users, or all exercise users unless the final recruitment strategy supports such a claim. The study is intended as an exploratory evaluation of the prototype and its two representation strategies in this specific XR rowing setup.
