\section{Study Design}
\label{sec:study_design_overview}

The study will compare two virtual rowing representation modes within the same physical rowing task. Each participant will row on the same ergometer setup in an ergometer-congruent mode and in a boat-realistic mode. The central manipulation is the relation between the participant's real movement and the virtual rowing representation.

The ergometer-congruent condition will prioritize close spatial and temporal correspondence between the real ergometer interaction and the virtual body, handle, and seat. The boat-realistic condition will prioritize a recognizable on-water rowing depiction with virtual oars and boat context, even where this requires a less literal transformation of the real ergometer handle movement.

The planned design is within-subjects: every participant will experience both modes. This is appropriate because the comparison concerns two alternative representations of the same physical action, and each participant can therefore serve as their own control. The condition order will be counterbalanced where possible to reduce order, learning, and fatigue effects.
