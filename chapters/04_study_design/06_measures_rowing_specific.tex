\section{Rowing-Specific and Experience Measures}
\label{sec:study_design_measures_rowing_specific}

The established embodiment items will be complemented by custom rowing-specific items. These items are necessary because the thesis does not compare embodiment in an abstract avatar task. It compares two ways of representing the same real ergometer action: one that preserves embodied action congruence with the physical machine, and one that restores a more recognizable boat-and-oar rowing depiction.

The custom per-condition items will therefore target the following thesis-specific dimensions:

\begin{itemize}
    \item embodied action congruence between the participant's real ergometer movement and the virtual rowing body,
    \item the real handle as a haptic and proprioceptive anchor for the seen virtual hands, handle, or oars,
    \item acceptability of the virtual hand/oar mapping, especially where the boat-centered mode retargets the real handle path into an oar-like action,
    \item sport-action plausibility of the rowing depiction, including whether the shown rowing action reads as rowing on water rather than only as generic movement,
    \item stroke-coupled locomotion and event plausibility, meaning whether virtual boat, body, or world movement appears to be caused by the participant's rowing effort,
    \item noticeable conflict, discomfort, or cybersickness symptoms as safety- and limitation-relevant observations.
\end{itemize}

These custom items will be treated as exploratory thesis-specific measures. They will not be mixed into VEQ Ownership or VEQ Agency scores, and negatively phrased mismatch or discomfort items will be reported separately unless a final scoring scheme is explicitly defined.

After both conditions, participants will answer comparative preference questions. These will ask which representation they preferred overall and which representation felt more convincing for rowing. Open comments will be collected to identify mismatch sources, comfort issues, and details that are not captured by numeric ratings.
