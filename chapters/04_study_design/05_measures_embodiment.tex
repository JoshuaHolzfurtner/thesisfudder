\section{Embodiment Measures}
\label{sec:study_design_measures_embodiment}

The primary embodiment outcomes will be sense of ownership and sense of agency. These constructs directly match the thesis question because the study asks whether the virtual rowing body and movement feel like the participant's own body and action under the two representation strategies.

The planned primary questionnaire basis is the Virtual Embodiment Questionnaire (VEQ), focusing on the Ownership/Acceptance and Control/Agency subscales. These subscales will be administered after each condition and scored separately. The VEQ Change subscale is not included as a primary measure because the study does not primarily manipulate perceived body shape or body-schema change. TODO:CITE
