\section{Embodiment Measures}
\label{sec:study_design_measures_embodiment}

The primary embodiment outcomes are sense of ownership and sense of agency. These constructs match the central comparison of the study: the ergometer-congruent mode is designed to make the visible rowing body closely follow the participant's actual movement, while the boat-realistic mode may transform the visible hand and oar motion in order to depict rowing on water. The study therefore asks not only which representation participants prefer, but whether the virtual rowing body and movement feel like their own body and action under each mapping strategy.

The planned primary questionnaire basis is the Virtual Embodiment Questionnaire (VEQ), focusing on the Ownership/Acceptance and Control/Agency subscales \parencite{rothLatoschik2020veq}. These subscales will be administered after each condition and scored separately. The VEQ Change subscale is not included as a primary outcome because the study does not primarily manipulate avatar body shape, body size, or body-schema change.

Because self-location is conceptually relevant but is not preserved as a final VEQ factor, the study includes exploratory body-location items adapted from avatar embodiment questionnaire practice \parencite{gonzalezFrancoPeck2018avatarEmbodiment}. These items are analyzed separately from the VEQ subscales. They are included because the two representation modes may differ not only in control and ownership, but also in whether the participant feels spatially aligned with the visible rowing body and hands.
% SOURCE-CHECK: MD Papers/gonzalez_franco_peck_2018_avatar_embodiment_questionnaire_markdown_bundle/gonzalez_franco_peck_2018_avatar_embodiment_questionnaire_thesis_notes.md

Embodiment will not be treated as a single undifferentiated score. Ownership, agency, and exploratory body-location ratings will be reported separately so that the two representation modes can produce different profiles. For example, a representation could feel more directly controllable without necessarily being preferred overall, or it could look more like on-water rowing while still producing a stronger sense of handle/hand mismatch.
