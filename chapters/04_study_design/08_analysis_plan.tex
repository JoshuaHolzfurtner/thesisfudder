\section{Analysis Plan}
\label{sec:study_design_analysis_plan}

The analysis will compare the ergometer-congruent and boat-realistic conditions within participants. The primary comparisons will be VEQ Ownership/Acceptance and VEQ Control/Agency. Exploratory comparisons will include body-location items, movement-match items, rowing realism, responsiveness, comfort, and preference.

Descriptive statistics will be reported for each condition and measure. These will include at minimum mean, median, standard deviation or interquartile range, and the within-participant difference where appropriate.

If the final sample size and response distributions support it, paired statistical tests will be used for the main within-subject comparisons. A paired t-test may be used for approximately symmetric interval-style scale differences; a Wilcoxon signed-rank test may be used as a non-parametric fallback. The chosen test must be stated before final reporting.
