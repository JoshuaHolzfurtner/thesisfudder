\section{Analysis Plan}
\label{sec:study_design_analysis_plan}

The analysis will compare the ergometer-congruent and boat-realistic conditions within participants. The primary comparisons are VEQ Ownership/Acceptance and VEQ Control/Agency. These outcomes directly address whether either representation better supports the subjective feeling that the virtual rowing body belongs to the participant and that its movements are caused and controlled by the participant's own rowing action.

Exploratory comparisons will include body-location items, embodied action congruence, physical handle anchoring, virtual hand/oar mapping acceptability, sport-action plausibility, mapping mismatch reports, and preference. These outcomes are not treated as validated embodiment subscales. Instead, they are used to interpret how the two representation strategies differ as rowing interfaces.

Descriptive statistics will be reported for each condition and measure. These will include at minimum the mean, median, standard deviation or interquartile range, and the within-participant difference where appropriate. Visual summaries may be used to show the direction and consistency of within-participant differences.

If the final sample size and response distributions support it, paired statistical tests will be used for the main within-subject comparisons. A paired t-test may be used for approximately symmetric interval-style scale differences; a Wilcoxon signed-rank test may be used as a non-parametric fallback. The chosen test must be stated before final reporting.

The interpretation will remain cautious. Higher ratings in one mode will be described as participant-reported differences in this prototype and study setup, not as proof that one representation is universally superior. The analysis is expected to be most informative when the measures diverge, for example if one condition is preferred or judged more rowing-like while the other is rated as more directly controllable.
