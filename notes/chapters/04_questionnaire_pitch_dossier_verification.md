## Paper Support Checked

Core measurement support:

- `rothLatoschik2020veq`: supports VEQ factors, item wording, 7-point response format, and mean scoring. Checked against VEQ thesis notes and clean-reading lines around the item/scoring tables.
- `gonzalezFrancoPeck2018avatarEmbodiment`: supports body-location/co-location as an avatar embodiment questionnaire category, with the caveat that it should not be overstated as a final validated gold standard.

Core embodiment construct support:

- `kilteniEtAl2012senseEmbodiment`: supports ownership, agency, and self-location as components of sense of embodiment, while keeping self-location distinct from presence.
- `kalckertEhrsson2012movingRubberHand`: supports separating ownership and agency because they can dissociate.
- `kalckertEhrsson2017ownershipOnset`: supports allowing a short stable exposure/familiarization period before asking ownership questions, without claiming that ownership is guaranteed.
- `mottelsonEtAl2023bodyOwnershipVr` and `girondiniEtAl2025humanBodiesVirtualWorlds`: useful optional review support, not necessary as the main basis for questionnaire scoring.

Rowing/custom-item support:

- `lamb1989kinematicComparison`: supports the claim that ergometer and on-water rowing are related but not identical, especially around arm/oar-related mechanics.
- `soperHume2004rowingTechnique`: supports rowing stroke/ergometer terminology and the ergometer versus on-water distinction.
- `rauterEtAl2013rowingTransfer`: supports the importance of oar-water interaction, oar handling, and rowing simulators as design context, not embodiment outcomes.

Cleanup/context support:

- `neumannEtAl2018interactiveVrSport`, `harrisEtAl2019visionForAction`, `slater2009placePlausibility`, and `skarbezEtAl2017presenceSurvey` are correctly framed as situating/terminology sources rather than questionnaire validation.
- The remaining imported paper families were checked via the master paper inventory and existing thesis notes. The dossier now includes a boundary section listing adjacent sources that should usually stay out of the Chapter 4 questionnaire section unless the author expands the scope.
