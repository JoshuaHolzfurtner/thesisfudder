# Chapter 6 — Discussion — Back of Mind

## Purpose

Interpret the results in light of the research questions and theoretical framework. Discuss whether and how visuomotor congruence or realistic sport depiction influenced embodiment. Relate the findings back to the concepts introduced in the theoretical background (e.g., SoO, SoA, SoL, RHI, multisensory congruence) and situate them within existing literature. Address limitations and propose explanations for unexpected or weak effects. Explore implications for the design of XR sport and training systems.

## Reader state before this chapter

The reader has seen the results but has not yet been told what they mean.

## Reader state after this chapter

The reader should understand the significance of the findings, how they answer (or fail to answer) the research questions, and what broader insights emerge. They should also be aware of the study’s limitations and potential future directions.

## Core claims

- Interpretation should match the strength of the evidence (e.g., avoid overgeneralizing from small effects).
- Findings can be connected to the cyborg’s dilemma, embodiment theory, and VR sports design.
- Limitations (sample size, prototype fidelity, study scope) inform the degree of confidence in conclusions.

## Allowed claims

- Offer plausible explanations for observed patterns, grounded in literature and the data.
- Relate findings to design recommendations for XR sports interfaces.

## Forbidden overclaims

- Do not assert causal mechanisms beyond what the data can support.
- Avoid claiming that the results apply to all sports or all VR interfaces.

## Required sources

- Theoretical literature on embodiment, multisensory integration, avatar realism, and the cyborg’s dilemma.
- Relevant findings from other VR sports/exergame studies.

## Required project details

- Reference specific study outcomes (e.g., differences in SoO or SoA) as evidence for interpretation.
- Discuss participant feedback in context.

## Open TODOs

- Identify possible confounding factors or alternative explanations.
- Consider how the findings might inform future design iterations.

## Tone

Analytical and reflective. Balance enthusiasm for interesting findings with caution about their generalizability. Clearly separate interpretation from speculation.
