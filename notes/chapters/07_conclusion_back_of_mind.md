# Chapter 7 — Conclusion — Back of Mind

## Purpose

Summarize the main contributions and findings of the thesis, revisit the research questions, and provide a concise answer based on the results. Highlight the implications for XR rowing and embodiment research. Outline limitations and propose directions for future work.

## Reader state before this chapter

The reader has seen the results and their interpretation. They expect a final synthesis and forward‑looking perspective.

## Reader state after this chapter

The reader should have a clear take‑home message about what was learned, why it matters, and what could come next. They should also understand the practical and theoretical contributions and limitations.

## Core claims

- The thesis developed and evaluated a VR rowing mechanism that contrasts congruence and realism.
- The user study provided insights into how design choices influence embodiment.
- The work contributes to XR sport design and embodiment research.

## Allowed claims

- Emphasize what was achieved relative to the original goals.
- Suggest promising avenues for further research (e.g., other sports, more complex avatars, different mapping strategies).

## Forbidden overclaims

- Do not claim that the findings generalize beyond contexts similar to the study or that they definitively answer all questions about embodiment in XR sports.
- Avoid implying that the study’s limitations are negligible.

## Required sources

- None specifically for the conclusion, but echo key literature points where relevant.

## Required project details

- Summarize the system implementation, study design, results, and interpretation in a few concise paragraphs.

## Open TODOs

- Formulate a succinct and compelling closing statement.
- Identify concrete next steps for implementation and research.

## Tone

Confident yet measured. Provide a sense of closure while inviting future exploration.
