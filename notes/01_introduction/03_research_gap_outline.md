# 03 Research Gap

## Purpose
Position the thesis between virtual embodiment research and VR sport/exercise systems.

## Key claims
- Embodiment research studies congruence, agency, ownership, and avatar representation.
- VR sport/exercise research often focuses on training, motivation, presence, or usability.
- Less work isolates the specific conflict between physical apparatus congruence and sport-realistic visual depiction.
- VR indoor rowing is a clear case for studying this conflict.

## Source anchors
- Kilteni / SoE source.
- Slater / presence source.
- Avatar embodiment questionnaire sources.
- VR sports systematic review.
- VR rowing / cycling / exercise studies.
- Rowing biomechanics sources.

## Needs citation
- Embodiment components.
- VR exercise/sport research.
- Existing VR rowing or cycling systems.

## Human review notes
- Avoid overclaiming “not studied at all.”
- Safer phrasing: “has received limited attention” or “has not been sufficiently isolated.”
