# 02 Problem Statement

## Purpose
Define the central design problem: VR rowing must choose between sensorimotor fidelity to the ergometer and sport-representational fidelity to on-water rowing.

## Key claims
- Ergometer-congruent representation supports physical/visual alignment.
- Boat-centric representation supports recognizable rowing imagery.
- The two may conflict because erg handle movement and oar/boat movement are not identical.
- Embodiment depends on more than graphical realism.

## Source anchors
- Embodiment / agency / ownership literature.
- Rowing biomechanics comparing ergometer and on-water rowing.
- VR sports / exergame representation literature.

## Needs citation
- Embodiment depends on multisensory and visuomotor congruence.
- Differences between ergometer and on-water rowing mechanics.
