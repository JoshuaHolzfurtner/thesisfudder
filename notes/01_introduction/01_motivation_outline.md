# 01 Motivation

## Purpose
Introduce indoor rowing as a physically rich but perceptually reduced activity, and motivate VR rowing as a case where embodiment, sport realism, and interaction fidelity meet.

## Key claims
- Indoor rowing involves coordinated whole-body movement.
- Ergometer rowing physically resembles rowing training but lacks the perceptual context of rowing on water.
- VR can reintroduce visual, spatial, and social context.
- VR rowing creates a trade-off between congruent representation of the ergometer and realistic depiction of boat rowing.
- This trade-off is relevant for virtual embodiment.
