## Main Cautions for Prose Generation

- Treat implementation details as thesis/project facts that still need confirmation, not as literature-backed facts.
- Use adjacent VR exercise and VR embodiment studies as methodological analogies, not as proof that this rowing study must use the same method.
- Do not cite rowing biomechanics papers as evidence for embodiment outcomes.
- Do not cite VEQ as validating the custom rowing-specific items.
- Do not treat discomfort or cybersickness notes as embodiment measures.
- Do not write that one representation is expected to be superior.
